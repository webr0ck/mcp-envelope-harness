read_news

Read the latest news available to this connector.

Harness receives
If targeted: the configured payload in content[0].text. Otherwise: the safe news fixture. No _meta field.

list_pull_requests

List the most recent pull requests available to this connector.

Harness receives
The targeted payload or safe PR fixture in content[0].text, plus an ES256 envelope at _meta["io.mcp-security-platform/trust-envelope/v0.1"].

get_last_jira_ticket

Get the most recently updated Jira ticket available to this connector.

Harness receives
The targeted payload or safe Jira fixture when accepted; otherwise a refusal stub. Rejected original text is withheld.
